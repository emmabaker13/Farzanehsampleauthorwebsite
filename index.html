The story behind Ascent

In the quiet of an attic,
a bigger world began.

In a village in Iran, where tradition defined a woman’s place, a young girl found a different world in books. Her family’s attic became a sanctuary—a place where imagination could reach beyond the boundaries of her everyday life.

But outside those walls, girls as young as thirteen were being forced into marriage. Farzaneh began to question the life laid out for her. At thirteen, she left home, carrying a fierce commitment to education and the belief that her story could be different.

Meet the author

Farzaneh

A writer. An entrepreneur. An advocate.
Above all, a woman who chose her own path.

Farzaneh grew up in a village in Iran, where strict adherence to Islamic traditions shaped her early life. Witnessing the undervaluation of women, particularly the forced marriages of young girls, ignited her rebellion against these norms.

Leaving home at thirteen, she faced numerous challenges but remained committed to her education. She later relocated to the United States, earned a Master’s Degree, and became a successful entrepreneur and the author of several books.

Today, she advocates for the prevention of forced marriages and child brides. Her journey is not defined only by what she endured, but by the life she built—and the possibilities she continues to champion for others.
